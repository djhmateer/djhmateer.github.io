## What is NocoDB

Nocodb is a spreadsheet like interface over a (usually) postgres database. Sometimes called a no-code database platform. Allows non-developers to create software.

It can be self hosted or there is a cloud offering.

It's commerical compatiots are:

- Airtable
- Monday
- Smartsheet

And OSS

- [Teable](https://github.com/teableio/teable) 21.7k stars, 1.3k forks. 11 hours ago 1st sept 26. - AGPL3 license. Free / Business. SSO, RBAC, Audit etc..

- [Baserow](https://github.com/baserow/baserow) 5.7k stars, 707 forks. 8 hours ago last update 1st sept 26. Open source. Premium / Advanced / Enterprise.

## TL;DR

- Excited about the flexibility that this style of app brings to end users. The ability to create relationships between data types (tables!) and for teams to do it themselves is great. This brings a more stuctured methodology to data gathering.

- Concerned about the new license for nocodb.

## Tables and Field Data Types

[![alt text](/assets/2026-09-01/2.jpg "ii")](/assets/2026-09-01/2.jpg)

Behind the scenes there is a `uidt` which is NocoDB's field type identifier. Here are the types used in Users

- SingleLineText - eg ReferenceID
- SingleSelect - eg Role on Users, Investigator / Analyst
- LongText - has richtext eg Biography in Users 
- Date - you can format it how you like in the system. I've gone with yyyy-mm-dd 
- Attachment - eg Image in Users
- Email - eg and we can enforce a real email address
- URL
- PhoneNumber - but it rejects space eg +44 7584 126022. So I've turned off validation.
- MultiSelect eg Specialism in Users
- Checkbox (boolean) - eg Active

Unique constraint values only on Business tiers.

No not NULL concept (except on a Form)

### Relationships 

LinkToAnotherRecord or LTAR (not Links which is now deprecated) is the `uidt` record type behind the scenes.

The concept of 1 to many, many to many.

### Computed Fields

- Lookup - gets value from linked table via LTAR
- Rollup - aggregates eg sum, count, average eg Exhibit Count from linked records in another table via LTAR
- Formula


## Licensing and Pricing

It used to be open source but now is a 'Sustainable Use License' which is what n8n (200k stars) who created the license use, sometimes called 'fair source'. Only 2 projects use this license to my knowledge.

On the 29th of Jan 2026 it was changed to "Sustainable Use License" which essentially means:

> You may use or modify the software only for your own internal business purposes OR for non-commercial OR personal use 

(capitals are me highlighting)

I asked for clarification from support@nocodb.com and got a great response back


```
Here is a breakdown regarding your specific points:

- Internal Business Purposes : In software licensing, "internal business purposes" applies to non-profits, charities, and NGOs just as it does to commercial enterprises. It refers to using the software inside the organization to conduct its operations and mission. Using NocoDB internally to track investigation evidence—restricted to authorized investigators and staff—is standard internal use.

- User Limits: The self-hosted Community Edition does not restrict the number of internal users, investigators, or records. A paid Business plan is not required simply because a larger team is entering data.

- Scope of Restriction: The core restriction in the license **prevents third parties from reselling NocoDB, offering it as a paid public SaaS product, or embedding it into commercial software for external resale**. Internal data management does not trigger these restrictions.

Additionally, should they wish to explore our paid plans or proprietary features in the future, we offer discounts for registered NGOs upon verification of their credentials. You or the team can schedule a call with us at https://cal.com/nocodb/sales to discuss this further.
```
